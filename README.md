# Polyglot Pocket

**PolyglotPocket** is a multi-user Android app for learning vocabulary one flashcard at a time: it shows you an English word and you type its translation, with instant feedback. You can study Spanish, French, Portuguese, Dutch or Arabic.

Here's what you can do with it:

- **Train in three ways** — with random cards, by reviewing your past mistakes or through a theme based on where you are, so you learn words that fit your surroundings.
- **Build cards from real life** — snap a photo and the app recognizes the objects, translates them and adds them to the shared catalog everyone can practice with.
- **Follow your progress** — through a practice streak, an activity calendar and per-language stats on your accuracy and words studied.
